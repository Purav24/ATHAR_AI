01 / THE MEANING
A NAME WITH A PURPOSE

Every legacy
leaves an athar.

In Arabic, athar (أثر) can mean a trace, an imprint, or a mark left behind. It is what remains after a moment has passed — a story, a tradition, a lesson carried between generations.

We believe technology should help protect these traces, not replace the people who give them meaning.
